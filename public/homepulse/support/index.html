Getting started

Allow Bluetooth when asked. Open Devices › Nearby and add the devices that matter as known devices. In Settings › Home Wi-Fi, set your home network so observations only count while you’re home.

Which devices work best?

Devices that keep the same Bluetooth identity: fitness bands, trackers, headphones paired with this iPhone, speakers and TVs. iPhones, Apple Watches and AirPods change their Bluetooth identity every few minutes, so HomePulse may lose track of them. Phones on the same Wi-Fi can appear in Nearby as Wi-Fi devices instead.

Why does it say “Uncertain”?

HomePulse only says “likely away” when it was able to monitor most of the last 30 minutes. While the app is in the background, iOS limits Bluetooth, so the estimate becomes uncertain. Open the app for regular scans.

Is this a security system?

No. HomePulse estimates presence from wireless devices. It can’t detect people, and it shouldn’t be relied on for safety or security.

Free and Premium

Free: up to 2 known devices and 7 days of history. Premium: unlimited devices, 30 days of history and CSV export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

To cancel a subscription, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device, or tap Manage Subscription in HomePulse’s Settings. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases.
