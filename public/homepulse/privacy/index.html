Privacy Policy

HomePulse – Home Monitor · Effective 2 November 2026

In short: HomePulse collects no personal data.
  • It observes nearby Bluetooth devices and devices on your Wi-Fi, on your iPhone or iPad.
  • What it observes is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What HomePulse observes

While monitoring, HomePulse records the Bluetooth devices it hears nearby: an identifier your device assigns to each one, the name the device advertises (if any), its signal strength and the time. With Local Network access it also notes the names of devices that announce themselves on your Wi-Fi, such as phones, speakers and TVs. It records whether it was able to scan, and whether the app was open or in the background.

Wi-Fi name and location

iOS only tells apps the name of the Wi-Fi network you’re on if they have location access. HomePulse uses the network name to know whether this device is at home. It never reads, records or shares your location.

Where it’s stored

Observations, known devices and their names stay on your device, for 7 days (30 with Premium). You can export them as a CSV file, which goes only where you send it, and clear them at any time in Settings. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they’re created on your device from what HomePulse observed. Nothing is sent from a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks what you have bought through Apple’s StoreKit, on your device.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
