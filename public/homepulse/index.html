HomePulse – Home Monitor

For iPhone and iPad

HomePulse turns the wireless devices already in your home into a motion watch, so you can sleep knowing your phone will tell you when something moves where it shouldn’t.

Your iPhone sits on the nightstand. Your iPad, a second iPhone, your AirPods and other Bluetooth devices become signal sources. HomePulse learns the normal signal between your iPhone and each of them, then alerts you when something crosses one of those paths. No cameras, no sensors to buy, nothing to install.

  • Use what you already have: an iPad or spare iPhone as a steady transmitter, AirPods and other Bluetooth devices, several paths at once.
  • Night Watch: a dark screen that dims itself and alerts you, with sound and vibration, when a path is crossed.
  • Signal radar: see every source around your iPhone, and which path changed.
  • Morning report: how much of the night was watched, every change with its time, and any gaps.
  • Private: no cameras, and everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

Good to know

HomePulse detects changes in wireless signals, so pets, doors and other moving objects can trigger it too, and it can miss movement outside a monitored path. Keep HomePulse open on its charger overnight: iOS only lets apps measure Bluetooth while they’re on screen. HomePulse is not a certified alarm system and doesn’t contact anyone for you.

Free and Premium

Free: sessions of up to 10 minutes, with your 5 newest sessions kept. Premium: all-night Night Watch, sessions of any length, every session kept, and CSV export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-month free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.
