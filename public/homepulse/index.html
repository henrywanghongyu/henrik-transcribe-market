HomePulse – Home Monitor

For iPhone and iPad

A calm read on home. HomePulse watches for the wireless devices you choose, such as a fitness band, a tracker or a speaker, and estimates whether they’re home. No cameras, no sensors to install.

  • A clear estimate: likely occupied, likely away or uncertain, with a confidence level and the reason behind it.
  • Monitoring health: it always shows whether it could actually observe, so a gap never reads as “away”.
  • Known devices: name the devices that matter and see when each was last observed.
  • Activity timeline of when devices were observed, with CSV export.
  • Private. Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no server, no tracking.

An estimate, not proof

HomePulse observes devices, not people. A phone left at home looks like someone home, and iOS limits Bluetooth while the app is in the background. HomePulse says so whenever it matters.

Free and Premium

Free: up to 2 known devices and 7 days of history. Premium: unlimited devices, 30 days of history and CSV export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.
