HomePulse – Home Monitor

For iPhone and iPad

A wireless motion lab. Place your iPhone and your AirPods, or a second iPhone or iPad, a few metres apart. HomePulse learns the normal Bluetooth signal between them, then watches for changes when someone walks through.

  • Honest about what iOS allows: it shows which signals it can actually measure, and which it can’t.
  • Calibration learns the normal level and variation of the signal before every session.
  • Live sensing with a signal chart, evidence quality and a clear status: signal change, no change, or uncertain.
  • Labels and a guided walk test, so you can see how well the changes match when someone really walked through.
  • Results and CSV export for every session, with re-analysis when the detector improves.
  • Private. Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.

An experiment, not a security system

HomePulse reports changes in a wireless signal. It doesn’t detect people, identify anyone, or say where they are, and it shouldn’t be relied on for safety or security.

Free and Premium

Free: sessions of up to 10 minutes, with your 5 newest sessions kept. Premium: sessions of any length, every session kept, and CSV export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-month free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.
