Privacy Policy

Henrik Video Generator for Mac · Last updated 7 October 2026

In short: everything happens on your Mac. Your voice, text, photos and videos never leave it. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer receives no data from it.

What the app does with your content

  • Voice narration is recorded with your permission and transcribed on your Mac by an on-device speech model (whisper.cpp).
  • Photos and audio you add are copied into the app's own private folder on your Mac so your projects keep working.
  • Storyboards, generated frames and videos are created on your Mac by on-device AI models and stored in the same private folder.
  • Nothing you create or add is uploaded anywhere. Exported videos go only where you save or share them.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only to download models and the AI engine when you ask it to, in Models & Downloads:

  • the speech model and the video models, from huggingface.co (Hugging Face);
  • the AI engine (the libraries the video models run on), from Google Drive (drive.usercontent.google.com).

These are ordinary file downloads. Like any web server, those services can see your IP address and the files requested, under their own privacy policies. No content of yours is sent. Generating videos works offline once the models are downloaded.

Data we collect

None. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting services and does not share data with third parties.

Diagnostic logs

The app writes troubleshooting logs on your Mac (Help › Show Logs in Finder). They stay there unless you choose to send them with a problem report.

Deleting your data

Delete a project in the app to remove its files. Delete downloaded models in Models & Downloads. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
