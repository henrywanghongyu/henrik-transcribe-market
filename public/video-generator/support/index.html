Turn your voice, text and photos into videos, entirely on your Mac.

Please include your Mac model, macOS version and app version (Henrik Video Generator › About). For problems, Help › Show Logs in Finder shows logs you can attach.

Getting started

  • Text & Voice → Video: record a narration or type the story, add photos, and click Generate Video. The app writes a storyboard and renders an .mp4 with captions and transitions.
  • Image → Video: add a photo, describe what should happen in it, and click Generate Video — the video model animates the photo.

Models & Downloads

  • Open it with ⇧⌘M (or Settings, ⌘,). Image → Video needs the AI engine (245 MB) and one video model: LTX-Video 2B (15.9 GB, fastest) or Wan 2.2 TI2V-5B by Alibaba (24.2 GB, 24 GB of memory or more recommended).
  • Downloads continue when the window is closed. Delete a model there to free the space.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 or later. 16 GB of memory works for LTX-Video; a 4-second clip takes a few minutes.

Privacy

Everything runs on your Mac and no data is collected. Read the privacy policy.
