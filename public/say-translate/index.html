Say & Translate – Voice

For iPhone and iPad

Speak naturally and understand each other. Say & Translate turns what you say into another language, as text and as a voice, right on your device. No account and no cloud.

  • Two-way conversations: each person taps their language and speaks. Live captions, then the translation read aloud.
  • Face to face: split the screen across a table so each of you reads your own language.
  • Text translation: type or paste, and hear the result.
  • History and Favorites, if you choose to save, with phrases on a Home Screen widget.
  • Works offline. Download the languages once; everything then runs on your device, even in Airplane Mode.
  • Private. What you say and translate never leaves your device.

Free and Premium

Free: voice and text translation for one language pair, the first one you translate. Premium: every language pair, face-to-face mode and conversation export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.
