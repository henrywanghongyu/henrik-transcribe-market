Getting started

Open Models on Wi-Fi and download your two languages. After that the app works without an internet connection, even in Airplane Mode.

Apple Built-in or Downloaded Models?

You choose in Models. Apple Built-in uses iOS’s own speech recognition and translation, shared with other apps, and works on any iPhone on iOS 26. Downloaded Models uses the app’s own packs: about 4 GB, on an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M-series chip.

How do I save a phrase?

Turn on Save Conversations in Settings to keep what you translate in History, and mark the phrases you use most as favorites. They also appear on the Home Screen widget.

Free and Premium

Free: voice and text translation for one language pair, the first one you translate. Premium unlocks every pair, face-to-face mode and conversation export, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

To cancel a subscription, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device, or tap Manage Subscription in the app’s Premium screen. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings.
