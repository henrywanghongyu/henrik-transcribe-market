Getting ready for an event

Before you travel, open Languages and download what the talk needs, over Wi-Fi: Voice & Language Detection, speech recognition for the speaker’s language, and translation into your language. Speech and translation are separate downloads. When the Interpreter page shows no notice, everything is ready and the app works in Airplane Mode.

Which engine should I choose?

Apple Built-in is the default. It needs little storage, and its languages are shared with other apps. Whisper is a 1.65 GB download into the app and needs an iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an M-series chip.

Why is there no voice?

Spoken interpretation plays through headphones. On the speaker, the microphone could hear the voice and translate it again, so it stays off unless you allow it in Settings. AirPods work well: the app records with the iPhone’s microphone and plays through the AirPods, so point your iPhone towards the speaker.

Free and Premium

Free: sessions of up to 15 minutes, as many as you like, with the transcript saved. Premium: sessions of any length, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

To cancel a subscription, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your device, or tap Manage Subscription in the app’s Settings. On a new device, tap Restore Purchases in the app’s Settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later.
