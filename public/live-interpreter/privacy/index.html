Privacy Policy

Live Conference Interpreter · Effective 30 October 2026

In short: Live Conference Interpreter collects no personal data.
  • Speech recognition, translation and spoken interpretation all run on your device.
  • The audio of a talk, its transcript and its translation are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Audio and transcripts

The app uses the microphone only while a session is running. The audio is turned into text and translated on your iPhone or iPad, and is not saved as a recording. Transcripts and translations are saved in Sessions, on your device only. You can delete them at any time, and deleting the app deletes them too.

Home Screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows the title, date, length and languages of your recent sessions. This summary stays on your device and contains no transcripts.

Downloads

Apple’s speech recognition and translation languages are downloaded by iOS, under Apple’s privacy policy. The app’s own models are downloaded when you tap Download in Languages. They come directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which receives the standard information any website receives with a download request, such as your IP address. Nothing you say or read is included. After that, the app works without an internet connection.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks what you have bought through Apple’s StoreKit, on your device.

Settings and diagnostics

Your languages and preferences are stored on your device. If you run sessions, the app also keeps a small technical log (delay, memory, temperature state and battery level) in its own folder on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
