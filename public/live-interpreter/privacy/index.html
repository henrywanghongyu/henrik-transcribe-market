Live Conference Interpreter Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Live Conference Interpreter · Effective 30 October 2026

In short: Live Conference Interpreter collects no personal data.
  • Speech recognition, translation and spoken interpretation all run on your device.
  • The audio of a talk, its transcript and its translation are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

The talks you listen to

The app uses the microphone only during a session you start. The audio is turned into text, translated and read aloud on your device, by Apple’s on-device speech recognition and translation, or by a speech model you downloaded. Audio is processed in short pieces and is not saved to a file.

Only use the app where you are allowed to. Some talks and events do not permit recording or transcription.

Saved sessions

Each session’s transcript and translation are stored on your device, so you can read them later. You can delete a session at any time, and deleting the app deletes them all. They leave your device only if you export or share them yourself.

Home Screen widget

If you add the widget, it shows session titles, dates, lengths and languages. This summary stays on your device and contains no transcripts.

Language and model downloads

Downloads happen only when you ask for them. Apple’s speech and translation languages are downloaded by iOS from Apple. The app’s other speech models come directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which receives the standard information any website receives with a download request, such as your IP address. After that, the app needs no internet connection, and it never falls back to a cloud service.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app checks what you have bought through Apple’s StoreKit, on your device.

Settings and diagnostics

Your languages and preferences are stored on your device. The app also writes simple diagnostics for each session (delay, memory, temperature state and battery level) to a file on your device, which you can see in the Files app. They contain no speech or text and are not sent anywhere.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
