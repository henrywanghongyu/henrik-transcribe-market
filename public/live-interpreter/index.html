Live Conference Interpreter

For iPhone and iPad

Follow a talk, lecture or seminar in a language you understand. Point your iPhone at the speaker and read live translated captions, or hear the interpretation in your headphones.

  • Live captions: the original, the translation, or both.
  • Spoken interpretation through your headphones or AirPods.
  • Automatic language detection, including speakers who switch language.
  • Works offline. Download the languages once; everything then runs on your device.
  • Saved sessions with the full transcript, for your notes.
  • Private. The talk’s audio and text never leave your device.

Free and Premium

Free: sessions of up to 15 minutes, as many as you like. Premium: sessions of any length, as a monthly or annual subscription (each with a one-week free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.
